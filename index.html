Meet the Pianist

Piano Performance Student & Experienced Instructor | RCM Level 10 Certified

I am a second-year university student specializing in Piano Performance, with a strong passion for both music education and performance. I began teaching piano at the age of 16 at the Ontario Conservatory of Music, where I gained valuable experience working with students of varying ages and skill levels. I have also completed my RCM Level 10 certification, demonstrating a high level of technical and musical proficiency.

I currently teach at the Marian School of Music while pursuing my degree, allowing me to combine academic training with hands-on teaching experience. My lessons focus on developing strong technical foundations, musical expression, and confidence, while fostering a genuine enjoyment of music.

I work with students of all levels and tailor each lesson to their individual goals—whether preparing for exams, building skills, or simply learning for enjoyment. I strive to create a supportive and engaging environment where students feel motivated to grow as musicians..
